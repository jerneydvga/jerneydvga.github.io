About Me

Drawing

When I was a kid, I was also obsessed with art. I drew, but I'm not really good at it. I just draw whatever is on my mind. I used to do calligraphy, so I have a lot of art supplies like calligraphy pens, fine liners, different shades of pencils, markers, Speedballs, and etc. I was an art nerd before, but I'm not really good at it. I just love collecting art supplies.

As I grew older, I stopped caring about art. Then, in 2021, I finally had the courage to draw again. I drew an influencer, and that was my first portrait ever. Then in 2022, I drew my Lola, and in 2023, I drew my Lolo. In 2024, I drew my crush and sent it to her. In 2025, I drew Haerin from NewJeans, and I also drew my crush again, but better this time. Now, in 2026, I'm currently drawing my cousins. There are 7 of us in one frame, and I'm planning on giving it to them in December.

I never studied how to draw. I just feel like it and figure things out on my own.

Violin

I started playing the violin in 2017. I really wasn't meant to play it, but then I got into it. I was forced to play it, and we were required to practice for 3 hours every day. Every Saturday, we also had a 3-hour lesson with Sir Dino.

So I didn't really get to enjoy my childhood until I got older. A lot of things happened during quarantine. We didn't get treated nicely back then. We would get scolded and shouted at, and I used to cry a lot when I was younger. That is why I became numb when I became a teenager.

As of now, I got to enjoy and love it. I've already accepted it, and now I appreciate playing the violin. We play at our church events, and we also play at outside events.

About My Attitude

I'm pretty numb since I got used to it and to everything. In terms of empathy, I'm really empathetic. I just don't think people deserve it sometimes, so I hold back and always try to ignore it since I've also experienced things before.

When I love something or someone, I give my very best. You'd be able to notice it. And when I hate or don't like someone, I show it to them, and you'd be able to feel it.

I don't take hints or anything. I want communication, and whatever you do means nothing to me unless you talk to me and are direct about it because I don't like assuming things.

I'm a "I don't care" person. I don't really care about anything unless I'm involved. I don't like greeting anyone unless we're that close. I also don't really have the initiative to do things, although I want to. I just forget sometimes, and I'm working on it, so don't be shy to tell me something when I should do it.

I love making things fun, so whenever you're with me, I always try my best to make sure everyone enjoys themselves. I also don't want anyone to be left out.

I'm a pretty cheap person, and I only spend my money when I really care about someone.
